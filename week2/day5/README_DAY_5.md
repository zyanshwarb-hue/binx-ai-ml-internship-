## 🎯 Objectives

- Perform bivariate analysis with scatter plots and grouped box plots.
- Compute and interpret a correlation matrix and heatmap.
- Assemble a complete, narrated EDA notebook on a real dataset.

---

## 🧠 Approach

1. Visualized the relationship between `age` and `fare` with a scatter plot, colored by survival.
2. Compared fare distributions across passenger class with a grouped box plot.
3. Compared age distributions across survival status with a grouped box plot.
4. Computed a full correlation matrix and visualized it as an annotated heatmap.
5. Used a pairplot to scan all key numeric relationships at once, split by survival.
6. Consolidated the week's full statistical summary into one reference table.
7. Wrote a complete data-storytelling narrative connecting every day of Week 2 into one coherent
   analysis.
